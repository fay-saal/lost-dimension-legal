# Lost Dimension Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Lost Dimension ("the Bot", "we", "us") is a Discord bot developed and operated by Faysal Ahmed. This policy explains what information the Bot uses, why, and what choices you have. By adding or using the Bot you agree to this policy.

Contact and support: https://discord.gg/YOUR_SUPPORT_SERVER_INVITE

## 1. Information the Bot uses

**Server information**
- Server ID, channel IDs, role IDs and the settings a server administrator configures, such as prefix, log channels, welcome message, anti-nuke, anti-raid and auto-moderation settings, whitelists, ticket setup, giveaway setup, and the 24/7 music channel.

**User information**
- Discord user IDs, and usernames or display names where needed to run a feature.
- Moderation records: warnings and cases, including the user ID, the moderator ID, the reason and the time.
- Giveaway entries, whitelist and extra owner lists, nickname requests, ticket records, and verification (captcha) status.

**Server backup data**
- If a server owner creates a backup, the Bot saves the server structure: server settings, roles, channels, categories, permission overwrites, emojis, ban list, and which roles each member had. Backups do not contain message history.

**Message content**
- The Bot reads message text to detect prefix commands and to run auto-moderation filters (spam, invite links, mass mentions, blocked words). This text is processed in memory to run the command or filter.
- The Bot does not store message content in its database.
- Logging features can post information such as deleted or edited messages into a log channel inside the server. That log lives in the server on Discord and is controlled by that server's administrators.

**Voice and music**
- Voice channel and server identifiers, the track queue, and the search text or links you give to play music.

**Technical logs**
- Error and diagnostic logs kept to fix bugs. They may contain IDs but not message content.

**What we do not collect**
- Passwords, email addresses, payment information, or private messages. The Bot does not use the Presence intent and does not track what users are playing or their online status.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide and run the features listed above.
- To protect servers from raids, nukes and abuse, and to carry out moderation actions configured by server administrators.
- To restore a server from a backup when its owner requests it.
- To fix bugs, prevent abuse of the Bot, and improve reliability.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, build marketing profiles, or use it to train machine learning or AI models.

## 3. Discord intents

- **Server Members intent:** used for welcome messages and autorole, anti-nuke and anti-raid protection, restoring member roles in backups, and moderation and voice tools that act on members.
- **Message Content intent:** used to read prefix commands and to run auto-moderation filters.

## 4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or rent data. Data is processed by the following services that are needed to run the Bot:

- **Discord**, the platform the Bot runs on.
- **MongoDB Atlas**, where settings, moderation records and backups are stored.
- **Our hosting provider**, where the Bot process runs.
- **Lavalink audio servers**, which stream music. To play music, the voice channel and server identifiers needed for the connection, and the search text or links you request, are sent to these servers. Some of them are operated by third parties.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the Bot.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Server data is kept while the Bot is in the server.
- If the Bot is removed from a server, we delete that server's data within 90 days.
- Server owners can delete their own backups with the backup delete command, and automatic backups keep only the latest few.
- You or your server owner can request deletion of data about you or your server at any time through our support server. We will act on valid requests within 30 days.

## 6. Security

We use access-restricted databases, keep secret keys out of public code, and limit who can access data. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in their country). The Bot is not intended for children under that age, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 8. Your choices

- Server administrators control which features are enabled and can remove the Bot at any time.
- You can ask us to access or delete data we hold about you through the support server.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
